# ADR-00X — [Decision in a few words]

Date: [YYYY-MM-DD] · Status: **Accepted** | Superseded by ADR-00Y

## Context

What forced a choice. The constraints as they actually were — including the ones that
turned out to matter more than expected. Someone reading this in a year needs to
understand the situation, not just the outcome.

## Decision

What we chose. State it plainly, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What this makes easy. What it makes hard. What we knowingly accepted as the cost.

Be specific about the downside. An ADR that lists only benefits is marketing, and the
next person will assume the trade-off was never considered.

## Revisit trigger

The specific, observable condition that reopens this.

Not "when it becomes a problem" — name the measurement, the threshold, or the event.
